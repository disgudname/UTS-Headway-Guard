On-Board Transfer Rules

By default /onboard picks transfers and connections by itself: any other route with a bus running that stops within 80 m, CAT and Connect stops within 80 m on a route running now, and the Afton Express by its timetable. Add a rule here to override that at a stop. Changes reach the buses on their next update, no redeploy needed.

  • Off: not shown and not said.
  • Show only: the pill stays on the screen, the voice doesn't say it.
  • Always: shown and said at this stop even when it isn't picked automatically, as long as that route or service has something running.
  • Leave Stop or Bus's route blank for "any". The most specific rule wins (stop + route, then stop, then route, then neither).
